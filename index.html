Captain America: Civil War

Captain America: Civil War is a 2016 American superhero movie directed by the Russo brothers, based on the Marvel Comics character Captain America.

The movie explores the conflict between different factions of the Avengers following the events of "The Avengers." It delves into themes of loyalty, duty, and the consequences of their actions.

plot summary:After another incident involving the Avengers results in collateral damage, political pressure mounts to install a system of accountability, headed by a governing body to oversee and direct the team. The new status quo fractures the Avengers, resulting in two camps, one led by Steve Rogers and his desire for the Avengers to remain free to defend humanity without government interference, and the other following Tony Stark's surprising decision to support government oversight and accountability.

Facts about the movies

  • Screen Time: Chris Evans (Captain America) had about 5 minutes more screen time than Robert Downey Jr. (Iron Man).
  • Pay Disparity: Robert Downey Jr. earned $40 million for the film, while Chris Evans was paid $15 million.
  • No CGI Stunt: The motorcycle highway stunt featuring Bucky Barnes used zero computer-generated imagery.
  • The Airport Battle: The iconic Stuttgard Airport fight sequence was shot using roughly 90% green screen.

Captain America: Civil War grossed a total of $1,155,046,416 worldwide during its theatrical run. It was the highest-grossing film of 2016 globally
